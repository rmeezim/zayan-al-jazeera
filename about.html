Our story

An earned reputation, built one project at a time.

We pride ourselves on our earned reputation within the building industry, and much of our work comes from personal recommendation from our clients.

Zayan Al-Jazeera began in 2009 as a small, hands-on team that believed renovation should be straightforward: do excellent work, communicate honestly, and finish what you start. That principle hasn't changed. Today we deliver complete construction, renovation and commercial fit-out projects — from foundations to framing, siding, roof and window installation, floors and finishing — for a roster of retail brands, developers and homeowners.

What has grown is our scale and our discipline: ISO 9001-certified processes, in-house tradespeople, dedicated project managers and a 24/7 service desk that keeps live sites and trading stores moving.
